Privacy Policy - Aivengers AI

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Aivengers AI uses the Meta Graph API to manage and publish authorized content to Facebook Pages.

Information We Access

The application may access Facebook Page information and permissions required to publish and manage content on pages that have been explicitly authorized by the page administrator.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide authorized publishing, content management, and automation functions requested by the page administrator.

Data Sharing

Aivengers AI does not sell, rent, or intentionally share personal information with third parties.

Access Tokens

Facebook access tokens and API credentials are used only for authorized API operations and are not intentionally disclosed to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain only the information necessary to operate the application's authorized functions.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of data associated with the application. Instructions are available on our Data Deletion page.

Contact
