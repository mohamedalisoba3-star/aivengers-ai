User Data Deletion - Aivengers AI

Last updated: October 5, 2026

If you have authorized Aivengers AI to access your Facebook Page and would like data associated with your use of the application to be deleted, you may request deletion at any time.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of data associated with Aivengers AI, please contact us through our official website:

https://aivengers-ai.pages.dev/

In your request, please identify the Facebook Page associated with your use of the application so that the relevant data can be located.

What Will Be Deleted

Any stored information associated with your authorized use of the application will be deleted when the request is processed, except where retention is required by law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, or compliance purposes.

Removing Facebook Permissions

You may also remove Aivengers AI's access through your Facebook account settings by removing the application's permissions.

Contact

For questions regarding data deletion or privacy, please contact Aivengers AI through our official website.
